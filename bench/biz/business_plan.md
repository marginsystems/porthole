# TaskPilot - Business Plan (v3)

## What we do
TaskPilot is a project-management SaaS for small marketing agencies (5-20 people). Kanban boards, client approval links, time tracking.

## Pricing
- Single plan: $9 per user per month, billed monthly. No annual plan, no free trial (we do a 15-minute demo instead).
- Target: 400 paying agencies by end of next year, avg 6 seats each.
- Cost to serve: about $3.10 per user per month (hosting, support tooling, Stripe fees).

## Go-to-market
- Founder-led outbound on LinkedIn, roughly 20 DMs a week.
- Plan to start a blog "when we have time". No SEO work yet.
- Partnership idea: freelancer communities. Nothing signed.

## Team
- Founder (full-time, does everything: sales, support, product).
- One part-time contract developer (15 h/week, $2,400/month).
- Founder takes no salary; personal runway about 7 months.

## Competition
We think we have no real competitors because our approval-links feature is unique.
(Asana, Trello, ClickUp, Monday are "too big and complicated for agencies".)

## Financial goals
- Reach $25k MRR in 12 months.
- Then raise a seed round of $1.5M.
